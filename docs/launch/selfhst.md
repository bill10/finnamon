- **Name:** Finnamon
- **URL:** `<repo>`
- **Category:** Personal finance
- **Licence:** MIT
- **Short description (one line):** Your money. Your financial AI. Turn Claude or Codex into your personal finance assistant.
- **Description:**

  Like Mint, but it warns you when something's off, sorts your transactions with AI, and draws any chart you ask for.
  Finnamon syncs your bank accounts through Plaid (your own keys) into SQLite on your own computer (Mac, Linux or Windows via WSL2),
  messages you on Telegram only when something needs you (a duplicate charge, a new subscription, a budget about to
  go over), and gives you a local dashboard where you ask your assistant (Claude Code or Codex) for any chart. Your data lives on your machine; no
  ads, no data selling, no Finnamon server. Requires a Claude subscription (Pro/Max) or a ChatGPT Plus plan or higher; `finnamon demo` runs a made-up household
  with no keys.
