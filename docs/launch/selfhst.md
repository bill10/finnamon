For Self-Host Weekly, the newsletter's new-software section. Submit at https://selfh.st/submit/ ("Submit Content").
Checked 2026-10-08: the form asks which publication (**Self-Host Weekly (Newsletter)**, or selfh.st/apps (Directory),
or an icon request), then a category (**Project Launch**: "Brand new software or hardware"), then the fields below. It
has no category, licence or tags field; those go in the description. Re-check its fields on the day.

- **Publication:** Self-Host Weekly (Newsletter)
- **Category:** Project Launch
- **Project Name:** Finnamon
- **Link:** https://github.com/bill10/finnamon
- **Does the project leverage AI to assist with development?** Yes
- **Description:**

  Your money. Your financial AI. Like Mint, but it warns you when something's off, sorts your transactions with AI,
  and draws any chart you ask for. Finnamon syncs your bank accounts through Plaid (your own keys) into SQLite on your
  own computer (Mac, Linux or Windows via WSL2), messages you on Telegram only when something needs you (a duplicate
  charge, a new subscription, a budget about to go over), and gives you a local dashboard where you ask your assistant
  (Claude Code or Codex) for any chart. For banks Plaid can't reach, Fetch by AI: you log in to the bank yourself and
  Claude downloads and imports its CSV export. Your data lives on your machine; no ads, no data selling, no Finnamon
  server. Personal finance, MIT licence. Requires a Claude subscription (Pro/Max) or a ChatGPT Plus plan or higher;
  `finnamon demo` runs a made-up household with no keys.

The selfh.st/apps directory is a separate submission through the same form ("Suggest an App"); worth doing once the
repo has been public for a while.
