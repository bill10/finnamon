Finnamon. Your money. Your financial AI.
Turn Claude or Codex into your personal finance assistant.
Like Mint, but it warns you when something's off, sorts your transactions with AI, and draws any chart you ask for.
On your own computer (Mac, Linux or Windows via WSL2), with no ads and no data selling, so nobody can shut it down.
It watches for you: it messages you only when something needs you: a duplicate charge, a new subscription, a budget about to go over.
A dashboard that's yours: ask "dining by month" or "everything over $500" and your assistant draws it.
Yours to keep: your data lives on your machine, with your own keys.
Try it in 30 seconds: finnamon demo (no keys). https://github.com/bill10/finnamon (MIT)
Story: I loved Mint: one clean place for all my accounts and transactions, no ads. Then it was shut down, and nothing else fit, so I built my own. Mint rarely told me when something was wrong, so I added alerts. And Mint came before AI, so I recategorized a lot by hand. Now the AI does it, and I can just ask how we're doing.
