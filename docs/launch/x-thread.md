3/
Mint rarely told me when something was wrong, so I added alerts. And Mint came before AI, so I recategorized a lot by hand. Now the AI does it, and I can just ask how we're doing.

4/
It watches for you: a Telegram message only when something needs you. A duplicate charge, a new subscription, a
budget about to go over.

And a dashboard that's yours: ask "dining by month" or "everything over $500" and your assistant draws it.
